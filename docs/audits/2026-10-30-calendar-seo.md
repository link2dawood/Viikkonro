# Calendar SEO optimization: 2026–2028

Date: 30 October 2026

## Scope

Improve the annual calendar and printable calendar landing pages while preserving the existing route structure and the separate week-list intent. The requested deliverable covers 2026, 2027, and 2028; the shared page templates apply the same improvements to all supported years.

Live query-to-page data was unavailable for this implementation. Existing target ownership was preserved:  is the general annual month grid,  serves printable/download intent, and  remains the week-list page.

## Changes

- Replaced “Viikkokalenteri” headings and opening copy on annual month-grid pages with calendar-overview wording consistent with the existing title and description.
- Added a direct summary of the year's 12 months, ISO week count, week numbers, Finnish public holidays, and flag days.
- Standardized printable page H1 and breadcrumb to “Tulostettava kalenteri {year}”; the lead now states its A4, free-to-use, download and content details.
- Kept the existing supported formats: browser print/PDF, ready-made PDF download, and Excel-compatible CSV. No unsupported format is claimed.
- Printable-page year navigation now stays within the printable page family.
- Added computed long-weekend and bridge-day planning details to annual month-grid pages, using existing Finland planner day rules and holiday data. Copy explains that workplace agreements may differ. Week references link to the corresponding week page.
- Revised annual and printable FAQ source functions. The rendered FAQs and their JSON-LD are generated from shared data functions.
- Applied changes through shared templates, so the 2026, 2027, and 2028 routes receive the same treatment.
